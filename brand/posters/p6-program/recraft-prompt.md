# P6 그림 · Recraft 프롬프트

P6(프로그램 안내 포스터)의 라임 패널에 들어갈 그림. 패널 비율은 1020×580(약 16:9).

## 설정

- Style: Vector illustration → Line art (없으면 Hand-drawn)
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Colors: #0526C5 하나. 배경은 투명(또는 #EAFDA1)
- Export: SVG (받으면 보드에 넣고 색 · 굵기를 맞춘다)

## Prompt

A warm, playful hand-drawn line illustration of six people sitting around one long rectangular table, sharing stories. Simple stick figures with round heads, dot eyes and small smiles, each with a different hairstyle: spiky hair, a top bun, short curls, long straight hair, a fringe. Four people sit behind the table facing the viewer; one person sits on a low stool at each end, leaning in. On the table: small paper story cards and a few cups of tea. The person in the middle is talking, and a small flower blooms out of a curved line from their mouth, as if the story is blossoming; the others listen with delight. Single-weight monoline strokes with rounded ends, slightly wobbly like a felt-tip pen. No shading, no fills except flat white on the paper cards. One color only: cobalt blue #0526C5 lines. Centered composition with generous empty space around the scene, wide 16:9.

## Negative prompt

text, letters, numbers, logo, gradient, shadow, 3D, realistic faces, detailed anatomy, extra colors, frame, border, background pattern
