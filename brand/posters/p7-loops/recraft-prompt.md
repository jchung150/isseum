# P7 고리 글자 · Recraft 프롬프트

P7의 큰 글자 ISSEUM만 Recraft로 그린다. 작은 안내 블록(ISSEUM PRESENTS, 날짜, 주소, 프로그램)은
보드에서 그대로 쓴다. 그림이 오면 큰 글자가 비워 둔 틈에 맞춰 블록 위치를 다시 잡는다.

## 설정

- Style: Vector illustration (없으면 Line art)
- Aspect ratio: 2:3 (A3에 가장 가깝다)
- Colors: #0526C5 하나, 배경 #EAFDA1
- Export: SVG

## Prompt

A bold typographic poster artwork: the letters "I S S E U M" drawn huge as single thick monoline strokes with rounded ends, arranged in three rows of two letters (IS / SE / UM). Each letter is hand-built like a continuous ribbon, with playful loops and curls where the stroke turns (a looped s, a round lowercase e with a curling tail, a u and an m with soft bends). Letters are tilted at different angles, slightly overlapping the poster edges and cropped by them, filling the whole sheet with rhythm. Leave a few irregular empty gaps between the letters for small text later. Flat cobalt blue #0526C5 strokes on a solid pale lime background #EAFDA1. No other elements.

## Negative prompt

small text, extra words, numbers, serif, outline, shadow, gradient, 3D, texture, extra colors, black, yellow, frame, border

## 팁

- 글자가 틀리게 나오면 "I S S E U M"을 "the six letters I, S, S, E, U, M in this order"로 바꿔 본다.
- 고리가 너무 많으면 `with one or two loops only`를 덧붙인다.
